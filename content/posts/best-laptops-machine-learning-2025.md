##  What to Look For in a Machine Learning Laptop

Before we dive in, here’s what matters most for scientific ML work:

- **RAM**: 16GB minimum; 32GB ideal for larger datasets
- **CPU**: Intel i7/i9 or AMD Ryzen 7/9 for multi-core performance
- **GPU**: A dedicated GPU (NVIDIA RTX preferred) if you’re training models locally
- **Storage**: At least 512GB SSD (1TB+ ideal for data-heavy workflows)
- **Display**: High-res (QHD/4K) for viewing graphs, maps, and data plots
